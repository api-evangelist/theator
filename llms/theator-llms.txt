# Theator

> Theator is a surgical intelligence platform that turns minimally invasive surgical video into structured, actionable intelligence for surgeons, hospital executives, and revenue-cycle teams. It processes operative video into procedural insights, outcomes data, and surgery-to-text documentation. Theator is a healthcare technology company backed by Insight Partners. No public developer API, SDK, or documentation surface has been identified as of this profile.
